# Design System for Cooperative Bank Website

## 1. Overview

This design system defines the visual language for a professional, trustworthy, and modern cooperative bank website. It is designed for a mobile-first, multilingual experience in English and Kannada, with strong emphasis on clarity, accessibility, customer trust, and institutional credibility.

The system is intended for a static informational website and supports the public-facing banking experience without requiring complex dynamic interactions.

---

## 2. Brand Direction

### Brand Personality
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Stable
- Community-focused
- Transparent
- Secure
- Modern but conservative

### Visual Tone
- Clean and structured
- Financially credible
- Highly readable
- Calm and reassuring
- Premium but accessible

---

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary Brand Colors

- Navy Blue: `#0F2B46`
  - Primary brand color
  - Used for headers, navigation, primary buttons, important sections, and trust-building areas

- Deep Teal: `#0E6B6B`
  - Secondary brand color
  - Used for CTAs, highlights, and supportive accent areas

- Bank Gold: `#C79A43`
  - Accent color
  - Used for emphasis, rate highlights, icons, and premium cues

### Supporting Colors

- Slate Gray: `#5B6471`
  - Secondary text and muted labels

- Off White: `#F6F8FA`
  - Page backgrounds and low-contrast panels

- White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Card backgrounds and content surfaces

- Success Green: `#1D8F5F`
  - Positive or success states

- Warning Amber: `#D98B1A`
  - Warning or attention states

- Error Red: `#C43D3D`
  - Error states and negative alerts

- Text Primary: `#1A1F2A`
  - Main body text

- Text Secondary: `#4B5563`
  - Supporting body text and metadata

- Border Neutral: `#D9E1E7`
  - Borders, dividers, and form outlines

### Color Usage Rules
- Use navy as the main foundation for trust and institutional authority.
- Use gold sparingly to provide elevated emphasis and highlight value.
- Use teal for secondary actions and supportive UI regions.
- Ensure strong contrast for text against backgrounds.
- Keep the palette consistent across all pages, including English and Kannada layouts.

---

## 4. Typography

### Font Families
- Primary English font: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Kannada font: `Noto Sans Kannada`, `Nirmala UI`, `sans-serif`
- Use a modern sans-serif family for web readability and strong financial brand perception.

### Type Scale

- Display 1: 48–56 px, line-height 1.1, weight 700
- Display 2: 36–40 px, line-height 1.2, weight 700
- H1: 32–36 px, line-height 1.2, weight 700
- H2: 26–30 px, line-height 1.3, weight 700
- H3: 22–24 px, line-height 1.4, weight 600
- H4: 18–20 px, line-height 1.5, weight 600
- Body Large: 18 px, line-height 1.7, weight 400
- Body Default: 16 px, line-height 1.7, weight 400
- Small Body: 14 px, line-height 1.6, weight 400
- Caption: 12–13 px, line-height 1.5, weight 500
- Label: 12–14 px, line-height 1.5, weight 600

### Typography Rules
- Keep headings concise and highly readable.
- Maintain consistent heading hierarchy across all pages.
- Use strong weight for page titles and product names.
- Avoid overly condensed or decorative fonts.
- Ensure Kannada text is legible, with enough line spacing and no cramped letter spacing.
- Limit line length to a comfortable reading width for body text.

---

## 5. Spacing System

Use an 8px spacing scale for consistency and predictability.

- 4px: XS
- 8px: SM
- 12px: MD
- 16px: BASE
- 24px: LG
- 32px: XL
- 40px: 2XL
- 48px: 3XL
- 64px: 4XL
- 80px: 5XL
- 96px: 6XL

### Usage Guidelines
- Small component gaps: 8–16px
- Form field spacing: 12–20px
- Card padding: 20–32px
- Section spacing: 48–80px
- Hero spacing: 80–120px

---

## 6. Layout Grid

### Grid System
- 12-column layout on desktop
- 6-column layout on tablet
- 1-column layout on mobile
- 24px gutters for desktop and tablet
- 16px gutters for mobile

### Container Widths
- Mobile content width: 100% with horizontal padding
- Tablet content width: 720–960px
- Desktop content width: max 1200px
- Narrow content blocks: 640–840px

### Layout Principles
- Keep content blocks aligned and visually balanced.
- Preserve generous whitespace to convey trust and clarity.
- Avoid crowded layouts on mobile and tablet.
- Use cards and content blocks consistently across product, contact, and branch pages.

---

## 7. Buttons

### Primary Button
- Background: Navy Blue `#0F2B46`
- Text: White
- Height: 44–52px
- Radius: 8px
- Hover: darkened navy
- Focus ring: gold or navy high-contrast outline

### Secondary Button
- Background: Deep Teal `#0E6B6B`
- Text: White
- Use for supportive or alternative CTAs

### Ghost Button
- Background: Transparent
- Border: 1px solid Navy Blue
- Text: Navy Blue
- Use for low-emphasis or secondary actions

### Button Sizes
- Small: 36–40px height
- Medium: 44–48px height
- Large: 52–56px height

### Button States
- Default
- Hover
- Active
- Disabled
- Focus-visible

### Button Rules
- Keep CTAs clear and action-oriented.
- Use only one primary action per section.
- Secondary actions should remain visually subordinate.
- Maintain sufficient tap targets on mobile.

---

## 8. Forms

### Form Design
- Field backgrounds: white
- Border: 1px solid `#D9E1E7`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 12–14px
- Text size: 16px
- Focus border: navy or teal
- Error border: red

### Label Style
- Weight: 600
- Size: 14–16px
- Use clear labels for all fields

### Validation States
- Default: neutral border
- Success: green border and message
- Error: red border and message
- Focus: navy or teal ring

### Form Layout
- Use vertical spacing between fields: 16–20px
- Group related fields into stacked or two-column layouts as needed
- Keep form labels visible and close to fields
- For mobile, prefer single-column stack layout

### Common Fields
- Name
- Phone number
- Email
- Subject
- Message
- Select lists and dropdowns

---

## 9. Cards

### Standard Card
- Background: white
- Border: 1px solid `#D9E1E7`
- Radius: 12px
- Shadow: subtle, 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 43, 70, 0.06)
- Padding: 20–24px

### Product Card
- Similar to standard card
- Include accent strip in gold or teal
- Best for product overview and service offerings

### Feature Card
- Include icon, title, description, and optional CTA
- Use for trust points and banking benefits

### Info Card
- Used for environmental trust blocks and local service information
- Slightly larger padding and strong hierarchy

### Card Rules
- Keep content within a clear hierarchy
- Use minimal shadows to avoid a busy appearance
- Maintain consistent padding and corner radius
- Card hover state should be subtle and professional

---

## 10. Tables

### Standard Table Style
- Header background: Navy Blue `#0F2B46`
- Header text: White
- Stripe rows: Off White `#F6F8FA`
- Border color: `#D9E1E7`
- Cell padding: 12–16px
- Font: 14–16px

### Table Use Cases
- Interest rate comparison
- Product summaries
- Branch service contact details
- Frequently asked questions in tabular context

### Table Rules
- Use simple borders and strong row separation
- Align numbers clearly
- Keep text minimal and scannable
- Use bold labels where needed

---

## 11. Navigation

### Header Navigation
- Background: White or off-white
- Height: 72–84px
- Padding: 16–24px horizontal
- Link typography: 14–16px, medium weight
- Active link: navy or teal with underline accent

### Navigation Items
- Home
- About Us
- Products
- Branches
- Contact
- Grievance

### Mobile Navigation
- Drawer-based menu
- Large tap targets
- Clear grouping for product categories
- Sticky header for convenience

### Language Switcher
- Placement: top right or header utility area
- Design: pill or segmented switch
- States: English and Kannada with active highlight

### Navigation Rules
- Keep navigation simple and shallow.
- Do not overload the top menu with too many items.
- Prioritize key user journeys and local actions.
- Ensure keyboard navigation and focus visibility.

---

## 12. Footer

### Footer Structure
- Primary area: dark navy or light neutral background
- Secondary links: policy and support links
- Contact block: address, phone, email, support hours

### Footer Design
- Background: Navy Blue or off-white with strong contrast
- Text color: white for dark background, dark navy for light background
- Divider lines: neutral border colors
- Spacing: comfortable and roomy without excess clutter

### Footer Content
- Logo/brand block
- Quick links
- Contact details
- Privacy policy, terms, disclaimer
- Social links (optional)

### Footer Rules
- Keep legal links visible and easy to find.
- Show contact details consistently across pages.
- Maintain strong contrast for accessibility.

### Info Alert
- Background: `#EAF4F4`
- Border: `#B7D9D9`
- Text: dark teal or navy
- Icon: informational symbol

### Warning Alert
- Background: `#FFF4E5`
- Border: `#EBC88D`
- Text: dark amber
- Use for caution or special notices

### Success Alert
- Background: `#EAF8F1`
- Border: `#B9E3CC`
- Text: green
- Use for positive updates or announcements

### Error Alert
- Background: `#FDECEC`
- Border: `#E7B0B0`
- Text: red
- Use for errors and critical attention items

### Alert Rules
- Include title or short summary

---

## 14. Interest Rate Tables

### Style Requirements
- Header row dark navy
- Body rows white with subtle alternating background
- Accent cell or highlight in gold or teal
- Bold numeric values
- Clear labeling for percentages or rates
- Short, readable headings

### Typography
- Rate card values should be visually prominent
- Use consistent alignment
- Keep language clear and legible in both English and Kannada

### Notification Rules
- Include note that rates are indicative and subject to change
- Keep the table readable on small screens by using horizontal scroll or stacked summary layout when needed

---

## 15. Gallery Cards

### Gallery Card Style
- White card with border
- Soft rounded corners: 12px
- Image area with fixed aspect ratio
- Captions below image
- Hover state: slight lift or zoom effect

### Layout
- Grid-based layout on desktop and tablet
- Single-column stack on mobile
- Uniform card height where possible

### Visual Guidance
- Images should feel clean and professional
- Keep captions short and informative
- Use strong visual consistency for event and community photo galleries

---

## 16. Mobile Design Rules

### Mobile-First Principles
- Start with a single-column layout on small screens
- Use larger tap targets for buttons and navigation
- Keep text sizes readable without zooming
- Preserve strong hierarchy with fewer elements per section
- Prioritize essential actions above the fold

### Mobile Rules
- Navigation collapses to simple hamburger or drawer layout
- Product cards stack vertically
- Buttons should be large enough for touch use
- Tables should either stack or allow horizontal scrolling
- Contact and grievance details should be easy to access without excessive scrolling

### Minimum Interactive Size
- Buttons and tap targets: minimum 44x44px
- Link spacing: at least 8px between items when grouped

---

## 17. Accessibility Guidelines

### WCAG 2.1 AA Standards
- Maintain color contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text
- Ensure text and controls remain accessible in both English and Kannada
- Provide visible focus states for keyboard users
- Use semantic headings and landmarks
- Ensure buttons, links, and inputs are clearly distinguishable
- Do not rely on color alone to convey meaning

### Accessibility Rules for This Site
- All text should be readable against background colors
- Ensure interactive elements have clear focus and hover states
- Use high-contrast buttons, especially for primary CTAs
- Keep images meaningful and provide alt text where needed
- Ensure forms have labels and accessible error messaging
- Use readable line height and spacing in Kannada content

### General Accessibility Principles
- Keep design simple and uncluttered
- Use strong hierarchy without excessive visual noise
- Use large-enough touch targets on mobile
- Avoid tiny text in tables and list items

---

## 18. Design System Summary

This design system supports a professional cooperative bank website that feels reliable, modern, and visually credible. It is intentionally conservative and accessible, with a clear emphasis on trust, clarity, and usability for English and Kannada audiences.

The design language is built to support:
- public banking communication
- product understanding
- branch and contact discoverability
- trust-building UI patterns
- multilingual accessibility
- mobile-first responsiveness

---

## 19. Implementation Notes

The design system should be applied consistently across:
- homepage
- product pages
- contact and grievance pages
- branch directory
- legal pages
- announcements and notices
- gallery and visual sections

This ensures a unified and confident identity across the entire cooperative bank website.
